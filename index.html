Who is Mile High Sips?

A pop-up soda bar with a sweet little soul.

We're a Denver-based mobile dirty soda pop-up serving custom-crafted flavored sodas, cold foams, and cotton-candy clouds at events all over the Front Range. From teacher-appreciation days to birthdays, weddings, fitness studios, and corporate parties — we bring the cute setup, the sweet sips, and the vibes.

  • Fully mobile setup
  • Custom menus per event
  • Photo-ready presentation
  • Friendly, fast service

FAQ

Pre-Flight Check

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks out for weekend events. Smaller weekday events often have shorter lead times — just ask.

Where do you serve? +

All over the Denver metro and Front Range. Travel fees may apply outside a 25-mile radius.

What does pricing look like? +

We offer per-drink pricing for open events and flat-rate packages for private parties. Send us your event details for a custom quote.

How much space do you need? +

An 8x8 ft space works great. We bring our own tables, signage, and ice — just point us to an outlet if it's available.

Can you customize the menu? +

Absolutely — themed drinks, signature names, sugar-free options, even color-matched cold foams. It's our favorite part.

Ready for take-off?

Tell us about your event and we'll get back within 24 hours.
